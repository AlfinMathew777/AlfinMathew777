## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🗺️ TasMap — *"the brain for Tasmania's economy"*
A living, drill-down map of every business in Tasmania (**Island → Region → Town → Street → Business**). Pulls locations from OpenStreetMap and identities from the national business register, then runs a scoring layer that finds businesses sitting in real customer demand that nobody can find online — **the gap is the product.**
**Every fact is traceable: what was verified, when, and how confident.**
`Python (standard library only)` · `SQLite` · `http.server` · 70+ modules

### 🔮 Market Oracle AI
A multi-agent prediction and research engine that coordinates a swarm of specialised analyst agents to turn raw signals into explainable forecasts. Its prediction core now powers TasMap's scoring layer.
`Python` · `Multi-agent orchestration` · `Explainable predictions`

### 🏨 Southern Horizons Hotel *(AI-Agent)*
A startup-ready, AI-powered luxury hotel booking platform: an **AI concierge** that guides guests to a Stripe checkout, a staff policy assistant backed by a vector knowledge base, and a manager dashboard — with role-based access across six roles.
`React 18` · `FastAPI` · `Google Gemini 2.0 Flash` · `ChromaDB` · `Stripe` · `JWT / RBAC`

### 💸 MoneyPlan
A private, **offline personal-finance command center** that runs entirely on your own machine — expense tracking, per-job income logging, a bill calendar, and a 13-week cash-flow forecast with a danger line. **Everything is encrypted on disk; nothing leaves the device.**
`Electron` · `Node.js (built-in crypto)` · `Vanilla JS` · `No cloud, no telemetry`
